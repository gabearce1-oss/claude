# FamilySearch Browse Protocol — TSK-001 & TSK-002
## Catalogs 704679 (Civil) & 704681 (Parish) — Sahuaripa / San Javier, Sonora

## CRITICAL GEOGRAPHIC NOTE

- **Catalog 704679** = Registros civiles, municipio de Sahuaripa, Sonora, 1868–1994
- **Catalog 704681** = Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe (Sahuaripa), 1781–1961

San Javier was historically administered under Sahuaripa municipality until State Law 109 (December 22, 1934) split off records. So San Javier records will appear inside the Sahuaripa municipal collection, not in a separate San Javier collection. This is why catalog 704679 is the correct target.

**Split-coverage caveat (1930–1934):** Per Claim GEO-003 (VERIFIED, confidence 9), for the 1930–1934 window records were split across THREE registries simultaneously — Hermosillo, Villa Pesqueira, and La Colorada — not just Sahuaripa. Nela's 1910 birth is safely inside the pre-split Sahuaripa window, but death-record searches for Francisco's siblings or Aviana that fall in 1930–1934 must check all three registries. Flag this in the search log.

## HOW TO ACCESS (Step by Step)

### Method A — Catalog Browse (Recommended for Image Browse)

1. Go to: familysearch.org
2. Sign in (free account required)
3. Click **Search → Catalog**
4. In the search box type: **704679** → press Enter
5. Click on "Registros civiles : municipio de Sahuaripa, Sonora, 1868-1994"
6. You will see a list of microfilm rolls — each roll covers a range of years
7. Click the camera icon next to any roll to view images directly
8. Browse page by page — look for surnames Terminel, Terminal, Sagasta, Aviana, Avina, Porchas
9. Repeat for catalog 704681 (parish records).

### Method B — Indexed Name Search (Faster but Incomplete)

1. Go to: familysearch.org/search
2. Select Mexico, Sonora, Civil Registration, 1861–1995
3. Enter surname: Terminel — click Search
4. Repeat for: Terminal / Sagasta / Aviana / Avina / Porchas
5. Filter results by Place: Sahuaripa or Sonora

Note: The index is incomplete for rural municipalities. If name search yields nothing, proceed to Method A (manual browse) — do NOT conclude the person does not appear in the records.

## WHAT TO LOOK FOR

### In Civil Registration (704679)

- **Nacimientos (Births):** Look for Terminel as father OR mother's surname
  - A record of Nela (María Manuela Terminel Sagasta) born ~August 7, 1910 would be the highest-value find in the entire genealogical pipe
- **Matrimonios (Marriages):** Look for Francisco Terminel + Aviana/Avina Sagasta
- **Defunciones (Deaths):** Look for any Terminel or Sagasta death records

### In Parish Records (704681)

**THIS IS THE INDIGENOUS IDENTITY PIPE TARGET.** Look for baptism entries containing any of these Spanish-language notations:

- **india** — the standard colonial/early republican notation for indigenous woman
- **indígena** — post-Reform era equivalent
- **de nación yaqui** — explicit tribal identification
- **de nación mayo** — Mayo tribal identification
- **pima** — Pima/Névome notation (the highest-probability match for Aviana Sagasta)
- **mestiza** — mixed heritage
- Any caste notation: **castiza, lobo, coyote** — these appear in earlier records
- Godparent surnames: Porchas, Torres, López appearing alongside Terminel/Sagasta

### Recording a Hit

When you find a relevant entry, record:

- Full page image (screenshot or download)
- Film number + image number (shown in URL or image viewer)
- Exact text transcription of the entry
- Any notation about ethnicity/caste/indigenous status
- **SHA-256 hash of the screenshot image file** (mirrors the deed chain — see TSK-006)
